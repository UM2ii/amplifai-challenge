Getting started

Follow these steps to register, access the challenge data, and submit your model.

Step 1 - Register

  1. Read the terms and conditions.
  2. Create accounts on Codabench and Hugging Face.
  3. Register for the challenge by completing the registration form.

Step 2 - Access data

Download the training and validation data from AMPLIFAI on Hugging Face. Approval will be communicated by email to your primary team contact.

Step 3 - Submit your model

Model submission is open for the evaluation phase on Codabench. This phase uses the private test set and counts toward your official team limit of 5 entries and the final leaderboard.

This is separate from the earlier validation phase, which evaluated submissions on the 59 public validation cases and reported results on the validation leaderboard only.

For submission instructions, refer to the submission instruction repository on GitHub.

Leaderboard

View rankings on the Results tab on Codabench. The validation leaderboard reflects validation-phase submissions; the official challenge leaderboard reflects evaluation-phase submissions on the test set. Each team may submit up to 5 entries during the evaluation phase.
