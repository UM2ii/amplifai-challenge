AMPLIFAI at MICCAI 2026

The AMPLIFAI challenge will be presented as an in-person satellite event session at MICCAI 2026 in Strasbourg, France. The session is scheduled for the opening two-hour block on the final day of satellite events.

Date Thursday, October 1, 2026
Time 8:00–10:00 (Strasbourg local time)
Location Amsterdam room (Ground floor), Strasbourg Convention Center
